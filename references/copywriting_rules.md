# Copywriting Rules - GovIntel Communication Engine

## 🗣️ Tone & Style Blueprint
*   **Grade Level:** Write strictly under a **5th-grade reading level**. Use plain, blunt, and direct English.
*   **Peer-to-Peer Positioning:** Sound like a practical subcontractor or an engineer, not a software salesperson trying to set up a meeting.
*   **The Hook Rule:** Open immediately with an observation tied to their specific operations or their federal contracting status. Never open with an introduction of who *you* are.

## 📱 Mobile/SMS Layout Formula (Under 3 Sentences)
> "Hi [POC Name], noticed [Company Name] is bidding on [Sector] federal jobs out of [Location]. We built an offline, local app that lets field estimators drop in a 300-page municipal RFP and extract material spec costs locally in 10 minutes instead of days. Worth a look for your estimating crew?"

## ✉️ Email Layout Formula (Zero-Pitch Framework)
> "Hi [POC Name],\n\nNoticed [Company Name] manages federal facility maintenance under NAICS [Code]. Government contracts require extreme data security, which makes sending internal work logs to public cloud tools like ChatGPT a major compliance risk.\n\nWe set up local, air-gapped language models that automate crew dispatching inside a private infrastructure—zero data leaves your local system.\n\nI put together a short technical sheet showing how this layout handles data isolation. Should I drop the file here?\n\nBest,\n[Your Name]"
